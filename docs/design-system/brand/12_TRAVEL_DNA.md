# Travel DNA

Reusable category marks in `TravelDna.tsx` — one family for:

Flights · Hotels · Packages · Experiences · Restaurants · Transportation · Visa · Insurance · Loyalty · Rewards · Maps · Timeline · Weather · Emergency

## Shared grammar

- 24×24 viewBox, 2px stroke, round caps
- Primary stroke: tide; accent tick: dune gold
- Labels in Arabic beside mark in grids; mark-only in dense lists

## Category tint (background wash only)

Muted fills at ≤12% opacity — never solid category rainbows.

## Connection test

If a new mark cannot sit in the same row as Flights/Hotels without looking foreign, redesign the mark — do not special-case its styles.
