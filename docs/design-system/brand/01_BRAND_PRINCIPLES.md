# Brand Principles

## North star

Rahhal is a **calm, intelligent travel companion** — Arabic-first, luxury without loudness, tech without coldness.

## Five principles

### 1. Instant recognition
One screenshot must read as Rahhal: teal–gold horizon light, soft dune geometry, the Orb as AI mark, glass that breathes like sea mist — not purple AI kitsch, not cream-serif “luxury template.”

### 2. Arabic elegance, global calm
RTL is native. Typography hierarchy respects Arabic rhythm. Motifs (horizon arcs, dune bands, compass ticks) evoke travel without calligraphy decoration or folkloric pastiche.

### 3. Calm intelligence
Motion is slow enough to feel expensive (≤350ms). AI states communicate *thinking*, not thrashing. Prefer pulse, orbit, and soft luminance over bounce and sparkle.

### 4. Ocean exploration + human warmth
Depth comes from layered mist and horizon gradients. Warmth comes from gold/amber accents and soft coral alerts — never neon, never sterile ice-blue alone.

### 5. One family, years of scale
Every new surface inherits signature patterns, glass, shadow, Orb language, and Travel DNA marks. No one-off styles that break the system.

## Anti-principles (never)

- Generic purple-to-indigo “AI” gradients
- Warm cream + terracotta “lifestyle” template
- Broadsheet newspaper layouts
- Cartoon characters, emoji-as-brand, stock suitcase icons as hero art
- Glow stacks that look like gaming UI
- Floating sticker badges on hero media
