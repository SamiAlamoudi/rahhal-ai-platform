## Tier matrix (illustrative · SAR 8000 · 2 travelers · 5 nights)

| Tier | Intent | Typical posture |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | Maximize savings | Lower hotel/flight shares, tighter activities |
| Balanced | Default midrange | Even flight/hotel mix + reserve |
| Premium | Comfort bias | Higher hotel share, modest flight upgrade |
| Luxury | Experience bias | Highest hotel share + larger reserve |
| Best Value | Score-led | Strong within-budget + quality balance |

---

## Trade-off examples

- **Hotel saves SAR X** — budget/best-value hotel share vs premium primary.
- **Flight costs more but saves Y hours** — premium/luxury flight uplift vs time.
- **Exceeds budget** — estimated total above traveler cap with flexible next steps.

---

## Flexible recovery when insufficient

1. Different dates  
2. Different hotel class  
3. Alternative airline  
4. Different airport  
5. Alternative destination  

Ordered by estimated savings against the overage.
